Privacy Policy for Flip Streak

Last updated: October 2025

Flip Streak respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All gameplay settings are stored locally on your device.

Data Collection

  • When submitting results to the global scoreboard, the app transmits anonymous gameplay data (such as your current streak count, chosen side, and an automatically generated random install ID).
  • This data contains no personal identifiers, names, emails, or location information.
  • The scoreboard data is used only to display aggregate totals and streak statistics shared by all players.

Internet Connectivity

  • An internet connection is only required for participating in the global scoreboard feature.
  • If offline, gameplay continues normally, and no data is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

  • The app uses a custom backend hosted on Cloudflare to process anonymous scoreboard data. Cloudflare acts only as the hosting provider and does not collect or use this data.
  • No analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking tools are used.

Children’s Privacy

Flip Streak contains no objectionable content and is suitable for all ages.
